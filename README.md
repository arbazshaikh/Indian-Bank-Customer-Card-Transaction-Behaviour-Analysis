# Indian-Bank-Analysis

This project analyzes Indian Bank's card portfolio to uncover how customers use their cards, where they spend, which channels they prefer, and where fraud risk is highest. By integrating Customer, Card, Merchant, and Transaction data into a single unified view, the analysis covers 100,000 transactions (₹1.64B in total spend) across 25,000 customers, 32,457 cards, 500 merchants, 15 states, and 15 merchant categories. The insights are delivered through two dashboards — one focused on executive and customer insights (spending patterns, channel preferences, and geographic trends) and one focused on risk and fraud analytics (tracking the 91.15% success rate, 4.80% decline rate, and 5.38% fraud flag rate) — enabling the bank to make data-driven decisions on customer engagement and fraud prevention.
